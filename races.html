Latest News

Porsche Wins 24 Hours of Le Mans

Porsche took the overall win at the 2023 24 Hours of Le Mans, marking the team's third consecutive victory at the iconic endurance race. Drivers Earl Bamber, Timo Bernhard, and Brendon Hartley led the charge in the #2 Porsche 919 Hybrid, beating out strong competition from Toyota and Audi.

Porsche Unveils New 911 RSR

Porsche has revealed its latest racing machine, the 911 RSR, which will compete in the 2024 FIA World Endurance Championship. The car features a mid-engine layout and a naturally aspirated flat-six engine, producing over 500 horsepower. Porsche hopes the car will continue its winning tradition in endurance racing.

Driver Profiles

Earl Bamber

Earl Bamber is a New Zealand racing driver who currently competes for Porsche in the FIA World Endurance Championship. He was part of the Porsche team that won the 24 Hours of Le Mans in 2015 and 2017.

Timo Bernhard

Timo Bernhard is a German racing driver who has been a Porsche works driver since 2002. He has won the 24 Hours of Le Mans twice with Porsche, and has also won the FIA World Endurance Championship and the Nürburgring 24 Hours.

Neel Jani

Neel Jani is a Swiss racing driver who joined the Porsche works team in 2013. He has won the 24 Hours of Le Mans with Porsche and has also competed in Formula E and the FIA World Endurance Championship.
