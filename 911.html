The New Porsche 911 is Here!

Discover the latest features and performance of the iconic sports car.

Design

The new 911 features a refreshed design with sharper lines and bolder proportions, while still staying true to its iconic silhouette.

Performance

With up to 640 horsepower, the new 911 delivers unparalleled performance, whether on the track or on the open road.

Technology

The new 911 is packed with advanced technology, including a new infotainment system and driver assistance features.

Aerodynamics

Porsche has made some changes to the exterior design of the 911 to improve aerodynamics, including a new front splitter, a redesigned rear wing, and a larger rear diffuser.

Hybrid Option

Porsche has announced plans to release a hybrid version of the 911, which is expected to feature a six-cylinder engine paired with an electric motor for improved fuel efficiency.
